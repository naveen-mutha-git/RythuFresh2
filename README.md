# 🥬 RythuFresh_Sec

### Fresh Vegetables. Easy Ordering. Trusted Service.

RythuFresh_Sec is a full-stack online vegetable ordering website built for our family-owned vegetable business in Secunderabad.

The project helps customers easily browse vegetables, view prices, add products to cart, and place orders online. It also provides an admin side to manage products and orders.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 👤 Customer Registration & Login
- 🥬 Browse Vegetables
- 💰 View Product Prices
- 🌿 View Health Benefits
- 🛒 Add to Cart
- ➕ Update Quantity
- 📦 Place Orders
- 📋 View Order History
- 👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard
- 🥬 Manage Vegetables
- 📦 Manage Orders
- 📱 Mobile-Friendly Design

🌍 Real-World Problem
-----------------------
Our family business was mainly taking vegetable orders through WhatsApp Business.
Managing multiple customer messages and orders manually was difficult.
So, I developed RythuFresh_Sec to provide customers with an easier online ordering experience and make business management more organized.
